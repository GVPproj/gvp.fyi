# Think About Life discography research

Research date: 2026-10-07. Implemented `src/data/releases/think-about-life.json`: **seven distinct titles across nine entries**, including two separately displayed Japanese album editions, with four local sleeve images. This is the Montreal band featuring Graham Van Pelt, Martin Cesar and Matt Shane, not similarly named artists. Identity is supported by the [archived Alien8 debut page](https://web.archive.org/web/20091010220318/http://www.alien8recordings.com/releases/think-about-life), the Bandcamp credits below and [Discogs artist 505942](https://api.discogs.com/artists/505942).

## Method and date policy

Followed the [Miracle Fortress inventory](miracle-fortress.md) and [Discogs edition audit](miracle-fortress-discogs.md): artist/label evidence for release history; Bandcamp `data-tralbum` for current tracks, durations, artwork and actual album IDs; Discogs artist releases, master versions and individual release records for historical coverage. Research performed with Python requests in a background agent.

**The four Bandcamp album objects all say August 27, 2020. That is not the original date of the two albums or necessarily the earliest circulation of the singles/EP.** Dates in the JSON describe the earliest supported issue/circulation of each title; descriptions explicitly identify the current digital edition when its artwork or tracks differ. Year-only evidence stays year-only. Discogs dates are release fields, not submission timestamps. No month/day was inferred from an upload date, rights year or an unknown date.

## Limitations and access notes

- The live Alien8 site refused HTTPS connections with and without `www`. Guessed Alien8 Bandcamp album pages returned 404. Archive CDX searches initially returned an empty result and then 503 errors, but direct dated product-page requests successfully resolved to the timestamped **2009/2010 label captures cited above**. The current Secret City artist-page guess returned 404 and is not an evidence source for this band.
- An Every Conversation archive index request returned 503. Primary label corroboration of Paul Cries, Remixes and the physical Sweet Sixteen editions remains a gap; their existence, dates, labels and exact physical track lists rely on community Discogs records and associated artifact scans. No exact day is claimed for the first two or the Japanese single.
- Black Champagne's artist-vs-Discogs test-pressing year conflict is unresolved. The JSON explicitly prefers the artist's **2007** recollection, while distinguishing the verified **2020-08-27** digital release. The originally proposed What's Your Rupture? issue should not be described as a completed commercial release. Discogs' old claim that the songs were never released elsewhere is superseded by the artist's Bandcamp release.
- The two-track Sweet Sixteen Bandcamp release is dated **2020-08-27**, not 2009. Its single entry deliberately combines **earliest title history** with a **clearly identified current listening edition**, as with the albums. UK physical remix naming alone does not prove its mastering/duration is identical to the current digital mix.
- Supplementary [Apple album search](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=think+about+life&entity=album&limit=100) identifies the two albums, but Family has a January 1, 2009 date; the original label's May 26 takes precedence. An HBO song search returned irrelevant matches, so no Apple link was invented. No additional Spotify/Tidal links verified. Actual audio playback was not tested.
- Supported inventory, **not a guarantee that no further demos, promos or self-releases exist**. Searches/artist indexes are snapshots, and excluded compilation appearances do not prove a track lacked a standalone release elsewhere.

## Follow-up: Japanese editions and Every Conversation

Rechecked on 2026-10-07; release JSON was unchanged during that research follow-up (see subsequent implementation below). The user's recollection is supported: the self-titled Japanese release belongs to **Every Conversation's catalogue**, not merely an unidentified Escalator edition. Both Japanese albums were already encountered but deliberately collapsed under the earlier **one entry per title** policy. This is missing edition-level presentation, not two newly discovered album titles. The following primary evidence supersedes the earlier primary-source gaps where noted.

### Album evidence

- **Think About Life:** freshly fetched [Discogs 2928904](https://api.discogs.com/releases/2928904) still lists Escalator Records, **XQAV-1068**, Japan, **2 CDs**, `2007-07-00`, fourteen tracks with no durations. However, the [archived label announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20070802121437/http://www.escalator.co.jp/00_mt/top.html#a001027) explicitly identifies **EVERYCD001 / XQAV-1068-9**, 2CD/14 tracks, **June 27, 2007 (planned)** (`2007.JUNE.27（予定）`). The same page's `a001020` notice calls EVERYCD001/002/003 “EVERY CONVERSATION ３作品、発売中です” — the three Every Conversation releases are on sale. Thus release existence and the Every Conversation attribution have primary corroboration; the actual day remains unresolved against Discogs' July dating. Do not silently replace July with the announced June date.
- Its label track list exactly matches Discogs' order: **disc 1:** Paul Cries; Bastian And The Boar; Commander Riker's Party; Fireworks; Money; In Her Hands; Serious Chords; What The Future Might Be; Slow-Motion Slam-Dunk From The Free-Throw Line; The Blue Sun. **Disc 2:** Going Deaf In The Summer; Testify; The Blue Sun (alternate); Money (GVP remix). Those four bonus titles are already in the selected Bandcamp/JSON edition, but that does not represent the Japanese two-disc packaging.
- **Family:** freshly fetched [Discogs 2928917](https://api.discogs.com/releases/2928917) lists Escalator Records, **XQAV-1087**, Japan, one CD, `2009-10-00`, eleven tracks. The [archived label announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20110719010000/http://www.escalator.co.jp/00_mt/top.html#a001510) supplies **EVERYCD006 / XQAV-1087**, eleven tracks, Japanese bonus track, wraparound obi/double paper jacket and **October 28, 2009 (planned)** (`2009.10.28発売（予定）`). Its order is Johanna; Havin My Baby; Sweet Sixteen; Young Hearts; The Wizzard; Set You On Fire; Sofa Bed; The Veldt; Nueva Nueva; Life of Crime; **Holesome (bonus track)**. Discogs instead spells tracks 5/11 **The Wizzzard / Whole Sum**; track 11 has no duration. Prefer the label's spelling in any future Japanese-edition description, retaining the discrepancy rather than claiming a newly discovered different recording. This bonus is absent from the current ten-track Family JSON.

**Attribution correction:** “Escalator only” is incomplete/misleading at imprint level. The label's own site documents the Every Conversation catalogue numbers; the debut announcement names Every Conversation explicitly. Escalator is nevertheless not an invented association: it hosts these announcements and the [artist's Bandcamp](https://thinkaboutlife.bandcamp.com/album/think-about-life-lp) says “Rereleased in Japan in 2007 by Escalator Records”. Recommend **Every Conversation (via Escalator)** with both catalogue identifiers, not a claim that Escalator had no involvement. These sources do not establish a precise legal distribution relationship.
