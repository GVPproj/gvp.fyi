Eternal Family is a unique and unusual streaming service, specilizing in obscure art films and cinema oddities. My work for them spanned streaming infrastructure, content workflows, introducing version control and buidling their CI/CD.

## Eternal Family Streaming Platform

I Contributed to an AWS streaming platform that lets content operators manage channel lineups through Google Sheets.

- Ported the channel scheduler from Python to modular TypeScript, with concurrent channel processing, persistent scheduling state and randomized, no-repeat programming.
- Added movie-ID-based matching across spreadsheet metadata and AWS scheduling, while preserving compatibility with existing title-based configurations.
- Connected Dropbox ingestion to the video-transcoding pipeline through updates to rclone and Fargate configuration.
- Brought existing Google Apps Script automation into version control with clasp and GitHub Actions, and improved infrastructure deployment workflows and architecture documentation.

## Eternal Family Reader, their publication

I customized the Misty theme for Eternal Family’s Ghost publication. Reworked the header and navigation, repositioned Ghost search, and added responsive styling to align the reading experience with Eternal’s branding, including typography, colors and calls to action.
