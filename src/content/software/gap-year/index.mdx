Gap Year is a story-focused point-and-click adventure from [Mythical Voltage](https://mythicalvoltage.com/), an independent game studio based in Toronto.

Set in 1992, it follows Vera Novak, a young scientist whose experiments with a homebrew ham radio put her in contact with cosmonaut Oleg Sergeev. The story unfolds through hand-painted comic book panels that expand, contract and rearrange as you explore her apartment. Four seasonal acts revisit the space over the course of a year, with branching dialogue and changes in the apartment reflecting the passage of time.

## My Role

I worked on audio programming for the game using FMOD and Unity with C# scripting. I also made the music!
